Discord Rules

Community Discord server rules and guidelines.

  1. 1

    Netiquette and Discord guidelines apply on the server without exception (with a grain of salt): https://discordapp.com/guidelines.

  2. 2

    Administrators have the right to take any action they deem appropriate depending on the situation. If there are doubts about a moderator's decision (only if it is not in accordance with the rules), please ask the moderator to contact an administrator regarding the matter.

  3. 3

    Mass-mentioning administrators is strictly prohibited and may result in a warning or mute, and in extreme cases, a ban.

  4. 4

    Evading punishments or bans, creating additional accounts, etc. will result in a permanent ban on all accounts.

  5. 5

    Advertising any content unrelated to our server is prohibited and will result in a mute or ban.

  6. 6

    Offensive or unmentionable nicknames must be changed. Failure to comply may, in extreme cases, result in a ban.

  7. 7

    Sharing private information, even regarding public figures, is strictly prohibited.

  8. 8

    Frequent trolling of others in voice chat, such as screaming, playing soundboard binds, etc., is prohibited.

  9. 9

    All matters not covered by these rules are subject to the moderator's subjective judgment, appropriate to the situation.
